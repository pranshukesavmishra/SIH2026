## 2. The five honesty questions

1. **Where did the simulation come from?** We wrote it — `src/fsoc_pat/`
   (~5,500 lines): `simulator.py` (sky, photon noise), `beacon.py`
   (trajectories incl. SGP4 on a real ISS TLE), `disturbance.py`
   (turbulence, vibration), `pipeline.py` (tracker), `control.py`. Not a
   downloaded framework.
2. **Where did the replay come from?** `tools/export_telemetry.py` ran the
   engine on three scenarios and logged every frame to
   `docs/media/telemetry_run.json` (+ turbulence, + decoys versions).
3. **Is the replay really our software's output?** Yes — same
   `run_scenario()` entry point as the desktop app, fixed seed 20261169;
   re-running the command reproduces identical numbers.
4. **Authentic vs simulated.** *Authentic external data:* the ISS orbital
   elements (TLE) and the published physics models (SGP4, link budget,
   turbulence — cited on slide 6). *Simulated:* the sky, camera, photons,
   disturbances — deliberately, that is what the problem statement asks
   for. *Computed:* every performance number.
5. **Can we say "measured"?** Yes — with one phrase attached:
   **"measured in our physics-based simulation against hidden ground
   truth."** Never imply a real optical link was measured.

## 5. Fifteen likely jury questions — short answers

1. **Tested on real hardware?** No — the problem statement explicitly asks
   for validation *without* optical hardware; that is the deliverable.
   The algorithms are hardware-ready: mount limits, latency and sensor
   noise are all modelled.
2. **Will simulation transfer to reality?** The sim models the hard parts
   (40 ms latency, noise, turbulence). Validating PAT in simulation first
   is standard practice at ISRO/NASA.
3. **Where exactly is the AI?** A 497-parameter neural verifier that
   confirms each lock, trained on data from our own simulator. The gate
   stays classical; benchmarked 0.957 vs 0.900 AUC.
4. **Why not deep learning for detection?** Real-time at 30 fps on CPU;
   classical CFAR is tunable and explainable — AI only where it earns
   its place.
5. **What is the beacon?** The other terminal's identity light — in the
   sim, a moving point source on a real ISS orbit, blinking at 4 Hz.
6. **Why 4 Hz?** Camera runs 30 fps → Nyquist limit 15 Hz; 4 Hz gives
   ~7 samples per blink cycle for clean detection.
7. **What if it locks the wrong light?** The blink gate scores impostors
   near zero; 0 wrong locks in 64 runs; 1 stray frame in 3,900 in the
   decoy run, recovered automatically.
8. **What is a µrad?** About 1 mm of sideways drift per km of distance.
   Our 198 µrad median is deep inside the ±3° field.
9. **How do you handle the 40 ms delay?** Smith predictor — an internal
   model of the mount runs ahead, so commands aim where the target WILL be.
10. **What happens when the beacon blinks off?** COAST: the tracker points
    from the motion model's prediction. That is why detection duty is
    ~50 % but lock is 98.3 %.
11. **How do we know the numbers aren't faked?** The tracker never sees
    the answer; the simulator grades it afterwards. One command
    regenerates everything; the repository is public.
12. **Is the website live or a recording?** An honest replay of a logged
    run of the real engine — its banner says exactly that. The live
    version is the desktop app.
13. **Scalability?** The same engine already runs UAV, ground and decoy
    scenarios — each is one YAML file.
14. **Cost?** Zero: open source, no GPU, no cloud, any laptop.
15. **Future roadmap?** Hardware-in-the-loop with a real camera and mount,
    fine-steering-mirror integration, multi-terminal handover.

## 6. Competition & uniqueness
Likely rival approaches: generic OpenCV trackers (no identity — decoys
kill them), MATLAB/Simulink models (not real-time, no app, licence cost),
pure deep-learning trackers (GPU + dataset + unexplainable), and
concept-only decks. Nobody at internal level typically brings all of:
closed-loop 30 fps + identity-by-modulation + latency compensation +
64-run honest campaign + open repository + live replay site.
