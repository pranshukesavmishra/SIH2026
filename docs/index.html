ZERODRIFT

FSOC-PAT COARSE ALIGNMENT · SIH 2026 · PS 26169 · ISRO

Two laser terminals must point at each other with micro-radian accuracy before they can talk. Our software finds the other terminal's blinking beacon in a noisy, moving sky and steers a virtual camera to hold it — the complete coarse-alignment stage, built, measured and open-source, with zero optical hardware.
