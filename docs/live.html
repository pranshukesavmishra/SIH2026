Find the beacon by how it blinks,
not by how bright it is.

This runs the coarse-acquisition principle live on your own camera. Every pixel is asked what fraction of its variance sits at the beacon's blink frequency — so a bright lamp, a face or a hand scores near zero, while a modulating source is found even when it is dimmer than everything around it. That is the same identity test the full simulator applies to a satellite beacon against stars, cloud and sun glint.

BEACON RATE

Must match the blink rate of your beacon. A band around this value is searched, so it does not need to be exact.

DETECTION TUNING

The right values depend on the room, so tune these live on-site rather than trusting a fixed default. Locks the wrong thing (a face, shirt, wall)? Raise brightness and/or depth. Won't lock the real beacon in a bright room? Lower them — starting with brightness.

MIN BRIGHTNESS 165
MIN DEPTH 0.30

RIG CALIBRATION

If the laser moves the wrong way relative to the beacon, the camera and servo axes are mirrored for your physical mount — flip these until it tracks correctly. Use TEST RIG first to confirm the servos move at all.

Camera field of view — how many degrees the full frame covers. This converts the beacon's pixel position into a real angle for the laser, so if the dot consistently lands too far from the beacon, lower these; if it doesn't move far enough, raise them.

FOV HORIZONTAL 60°
FOV VERTICAL 45°

Once the laser is mounted permanently: the camera and laser sit at slightly different points, so even a perfect track lands the dot a little off the beacon (parallax). Lock onto a real beacon, then nudge these until the dot sits exactly on it — that offset stays fixed after that.

STEP 01

Press START CAMERA and allow access. You will see your live feed with a status bar.

STEP 02

Make a beacon. Open this same page on a phone and press USE AS BEACON — its screen will flash at the selected rate. A strobe app or any blinking LED works too.

STEP 03

Hold the beacon in view, roughly 1–3 m away. Within a couple of seconds the status turns LOCKED and a ring marks it — even with brighter objects in frame.

STEP 04 · OPTIONAL

Press CONNECT RIG to drive the physical pan-tilt laser straight from this page over USB. Chrome or Edge only.
